## Known limitations

The low-dose sweep is simulated from high-SNR public images with a known
Gaussian PSF and Poisson noise. The target is the high-SNR reference, which is
itself diffraction-limited, so the network removes an additional synthetic
Gaussian blur rather than the microscope PSF. The AI4Life-MDC25 real pairs
support restoration within a matched acquisition source, but they are not a
controlled low-dose series and the same weights fail on the large BBBC006
source in the same release. Background structure, aberrations, and depth-varying
PSF are not included. The absolute P values are full-scale units; mean
detected photons are 0.36/3.56/35.6 per pixel at P=3/30/300 (`results/photon_units.json`). External
gains are smaller than in-domain gains. Learned restoration can hallucinate;
uncertainty is a quality-control signal, not a guarantee.
